Data sources and licenses

Mathswitch is an academic, non-profit research project. We collect names, short descriptions, identifiers and links of mathematical concepts from the sources below, and use them to build a knowledge graph that connects the same concept appearing in different sources. Every entry on this site links back to its original page in the source. All content remains the work of the respective authors and is used under the licenses listed below.
